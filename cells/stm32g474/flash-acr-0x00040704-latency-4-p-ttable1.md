### Where the line offset came from

DERIVED, not chosen: 0x08001f00 mod 8 = 0. The last round of every implementation in this rig reads the 256 B byte S-box, so the entry width is one byte and the offset is SBOX's address inside the flash row. Source: arm-none-eabi-nm fw.elf: SBOX 0x08001f00. Choosing this number instead of deriving it is what put the G474 at off 4 and the nRF52840 at off 0, each carried over from another part.
