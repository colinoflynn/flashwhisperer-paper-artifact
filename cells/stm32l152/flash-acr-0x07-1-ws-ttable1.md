### Where the line offset came from

DERIVED: SBOX 0x08001400 mod 8 = 0, so line_off 0. The L152RE's flash read word is 8 bytes and every layout's final round reads the 256 B byte S-box, so the entries are one byte and line_shift is 3 -- predicted rather than swept, research/targets/stm32l152.md §3. Both table addresses are pinned by the linker (sbox 0x08001400, te0 0x08000400) and reported by the firmware's M command, so the address this trace count belongs to is on record.
